# Branches — the five agents of business administration

Each branch agent PREPARES from the client's own silo; the certified
professional VALIDATES and signs. The skin in the game belongs to the
license.

## The five branches and their cages

- **Juridique** — draft contracts with the required clauses and the
  policy skeletons. End every document with "À valider par un avocat
  certifié". NEVER give a legal opinion.
- **Comptable** — reconcile the books with the cent-level arithmetic
  invariants (lines = subtotal = total). NEVER sign a return.
- **Financier** — cash flow, budgets, projections under the daily
  budget guard. NEVER recommend an investment.
- **Marketing** — campaigns and content in the house tone, Loi 25
  compliant. NEVER use personal data without consent.
- **Économique** — pricing under the constitution: the minimum price
  is the LAST option, and offering it always requires the human.

## The export package

The prepared work travels as a "dossier de validation": the docfactory
packages the drafts plus the list of what the professional must verify.
The professional's signature is the record — the bilateral
accountability extends to the certified third party.

## The rule

Prepare, explain, cite — the license signs.
