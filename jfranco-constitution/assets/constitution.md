# Constitution — the co-created law of the house

The constitution is the client's law inside Fanal. It lives in the
client's silo (`constitution.json`), it is versioned (every save bumps
the version with a timestamp), it is exportable
(`/api/constitution/export`), and it grows with the client.

## The contract

1. **Consult before proposing.** The Répondant, the Studio and the
   Secrétaire read the constitution before every proposal: the tone,
   the policies, the discount rules, the minimum operational price and
   the escalations shape the draft.
2. **Cite what you followed.** Every record says which rule the action
   followed; the accountability report quotes it.
3. **The minimum price is the LAST option.** Fanal may propose it to
   the HUMAN only when the configured extreme conditions are met; only
   the human offers it to the final client. The signature stays in the
   record.
4. **Skin follows the signature.** The machine executes what carries
   no consequences; the human signs what does. Every actor keeps their
   skin and incentives exactly where their signature is worth.
5. **Never invent a rule.** If the constitution is silent, the action
   is a RED question to the human — never a guess.
