## 0. The thesis (the commercial lens)

Fanal does not sell technology. It sells **the difference the
owner feels on the ledger**: the reconciliation hours not billed, the
lost payments recovered, the double-entry errors avoided, the second
pair of eyes always present. Every feature we ship must answer one
question: *what does the owner stop paying for?* Consumption is the
business; the counter-register is the proof.

## 1. How software companies organize — and the AI-era adaptation

Traditional orgs split into: engineering, product, design, QA/DevOps,
marketing, sales, customer success, finance, legal, HR. Each layer
employs people who DO the work and people who CHECK the work.

The AI-era model (see [YC's AI-native playbook](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/OX-the-playbook-for-building-an-ai-native-company), [Bain on AI orgs](https://www.bain.com/insights/how-will-ai-change-software-organizations/), [agent-native roles](https://github.com/sou350121/Agent-Playbook/blob/60e7b9e049c5a142df9ed913583dd6e5680621e6/theory/05-strategy/agent-native-org-roles.md)) shrinks the
DOING layer and grows the DIRECTING and VERIFYING layers: AI agents sit
on the org chart; humans decide, verify, and sell. New roles appear:
skill/prompt engineering, evals & quality guards, AI ops, trust.

**We do not copy either model — we have already LIVED the third one.**
Our product IS the proof: one director, agentic executors, seven organs,
contract guards instead of QA armies, the knowledge ledger instead of tribal
knowledge. The company will run on the SAME organism model as the
product. That is the handwriting analogy resolved: we do not write by
hand and retype into the computer — we write directly in the machine.

## 2. The company as an organism: four clusters

| Cluster | Function | The product organs that already do this |
|---|---|---|
| 🧠 **PRODUCT** — build | Engineering + QA + design | workflow-master, decision-router, accountability-auditor, the guard gates |
| 🔥 **DEMAND** — sell | Marketing, sales, onboarding, pilots | realpolitik rule: consumption first; the demo, the tunnel, the one-pager |
| 🛡️ **TRUST** — keep | Finance, accounting, legal, customer success | the virtual register + Z-report (accounting already built), audit organ |
| 🌱 **FUEL** — grow | R&D, learning, people | autonomy-grader (selection), forensics (archaeology), Knowledge Ledger (memory) |

Every cluster runs the same loop: **act → verify → grade → consolidate →
archive**. The organs are not just product features — they are the
company's operating system.

## 3. The old process → the AI-era process (adapted, not copied)

| Old way (write by hand, then retype) | Our way (write in the machine) |
|---|---|
| Specs and tickets | Directives in plain language + the master prompt template |
| QA team testing by hand | api-smoke + bootflow contracts + strict smoke (the 5 gate layers) |
| Post-mortem meetings | Knowledge Ledger-LOG canonical lessons (append-only) |
| Knowledge base docs nobody reads | forensics dossiers + the audit organ teaching in the moment |
| Support triage spreadsheets | the audit organ (friction → teach → record) |
| Accounting spreadsheets | the virtual register + Z-report (counter-audit) |
| Code archaeology by senior engineers | /forensics (strata → trace → fossil record → dossier) |
| Hiring doers per department | Hiring DIRECTORS; the doing layer is the AI + the organs |
