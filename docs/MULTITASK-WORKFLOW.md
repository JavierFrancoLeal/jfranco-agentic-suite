# MULTITASK-WORKFLOW — the person's day, one task at a time (the design)

*How a person's many simultaneous tasks work in Fanal: the sessions,
the lifecycle, the closing question, the daily report, the permissions,
and the two-colleagues question. 2026-11-06.*

## The model (how the big software and DSH solve it)

- Microsoft 365 / Google: a task is a DOCUMENT or a LIST ITEM with an
  OWNER, a state and a timestamp. Simultaneous people = simultaneous
  documents, each owned; the shared layer (a Team site, a shared
  Drive) is what the team sees together.
- DSH: one session = one conversation, with its own lifecycle.
- Fanal adopts BOTH: **one SESSION per TASK, owned by one worker**;
  the worker's screen shows their OWN day by default; the shared wall
  (the live board + the kitchen orders) is what the team sees together.

## The waitress with many open tables

One session per ORDER (table/client). The worker can open several in
parallel; each has its own transcript and its own semáforo. The
worker's default view = today's list of their sessions (the "day
board"); switching tables = switching sessions (like tabs). Nothing of
the other workers appears unless the role grants it.

## The lifecycle (start → close → resume)

1. **Start** — the worker presses LISTEN or opens a new order: a
   session is born (worker, table, startedAt).
2. **Life** — the conversation flows; Fanal captures, proposes,
   verifies; the semáforo grades each action.
3. **The closing trigger** — the worker asks for the bill ("l'addition,
   s'il vous plaît"): Fanal detects the end and shows the
   question-action: "Désirez-vous autre chose ?" — YES / NO, with NO
   pre-selected.
4. **The auto-NO** — after 10 seconds without an answer, NO activates
   automatically: the task is closed, and the footer says: "La tâche
   est terminée — pour la reprendre: [the resume path]."
5. **Resume** — the session never dies: it stays in today's list as
   CLOSED, re-openable with one tap; the transcript continues where it
   stopped.

## The daily report and the permissions

- Every report generated by the field workers is stored in the CENTRAL
  brain; only the owner and the manager can open it.
- The daily PDF is built by Fanal from the day's sessions: the full
  conversations (the client's life stories included) — the COMPLETE
  record, while the live screens show only the relevant slice (the
  order, not the life).
- The worker sees only THEIR OWN sessions; the manager sees all; the
  owner sees everything plus the daily PDFs.

## The two colleagues at the same time

Production and management activate Fanal simultaneously. The design:
their sessions are INDEPENDENT (each owns their tasks); the shared
layer is append-only (the wall, the orders board, the reports) — like
two people editing DIFFERENT documents in a shared Drive: no conflict
by construction. When they need to touch the SAME task (a handover),
the semáforo governs: the task has ONE owner at a time; a transfer is
a GREEN action recorded with who-took-over-when.

## The KPI subtlety (the adoption target)

The closing question, the auto-NO and the resume path exist to MINIMIZE
human intervention: the worker never "manages" the tool — the tool
manages the tasks. Every correction the human makes that Fanal could
have made counts NEGATIVE in the KPI; every second saved counts
POSITIVE. The design goal: the worker's only necessary touches are the
YES and the signature.
