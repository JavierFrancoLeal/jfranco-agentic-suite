# FRICTION-HONESTY — when Fanal serves, and when it is pure friction

*The politician-tells-the-truth doctrine: in the AI era everything is
measurable, so Fanal must say when it is NOT worth it. 2026-11-07.*

## The semi-empty restaurant (the director's real case)

Five tables, one waitress, paper then the computer. Fanal there is
FRICTION: the volume does not hurt, the double entry is not a pain.
Fanal is the EXOSKELETON for the heavy day — not the uniform for every
day. On the quiet Tuesday it sits at zero CPU, silent; the user simply
does not touch it.

## When Fanal serves (the relief conditions)

1. The VOLUME hurts: the Friday night with 10 tables + the phone in 3
   languages — the double entry the director saw becomes ONE dictation.
2. The RECURRING flows exist: reservations, messages, forms, shifts —
   the same work every day.
3. The COMPLIANCE bites: allergies, the minimum price, the Loi 25 —
   the semáforo guards what the tired human forgets.
4. The PROFESSIONAL prep exists: the dossiers for the accountant, the
   lawyer, the inspector.

## When Fanal is pure friction (the honest disqualification)

1. Tiny volume (a semi-empty room, a solo operator, a one-off).
2. No message inflow and no recurring workflows.
3. The client who would rather keep the paper — respect it.

## The measurable honesty (in the code)

/papi/pilot/health returns the honest verdict: under ~8 governed
decisions a day → NOT_YET_WORTH_IT ("Fanal aún no te ahorra más de lo
que cuesta — vuelve cuando el volumen crezca"); 8-30 → WORTH_IT_ON_BUSY_DAYS;
30+ → CLEARLY_WORTH_IT. The sales pitch that disqualifies itself is the
trust machine: we sell where the numbers say yes, and we SAY IT when
they say no.

## The antifragile application

Fanal degrades gracefully: zero CPU, zero noise, zero presence until
the volume calls it. The client who bought it for the heavy nights
keeps it silent on the quiet ones — and the honest verdict tells them
which is which. Adoption = only where the relief is measurable; the
friction cases are told to wait. That honesty compounds: every client
knows we will never sell them a uniform for a day they do not have.

## The low-entropy standby and the target filter (Argon's answer, adopted)

- The CALM_STANDBY mode (built): with ≤2 open sessions and no alert or
  handoff, Fanal says it out loud ("no dictes aquí — usa tu flujo
  directo; Fanal vigila el teléfono y los relevos") and gets out of the
  way. The shield activates for the asymmetric risks (a foreign
  language, a critical allergy, a handoff) regardless of the volume.
- The clipboard bridge (built): for the closed SaaS without an API, the
  [⚡ inject] button formats the payload (TAB-separated cells or the
  structured note) so ONE Ctrl+V fills the legacy system — zero double
  capture.
- The target filter: pilot clients need ≥3 employees, ≥25
  transactions/day or ≥5 dossiers/week. The micro-business (the owner
  alone with 3 tables) is told honestly: "no necesitas Fanal".
- The brutal honesty line: the daily PDF reports the hours in calm mode
  ("3.5 h where your team operated directly without the AI") — when the
  owner sees we never inflate the quiet hours, they believe the 4.2
  saved hours of the Friday peak at 100%.
