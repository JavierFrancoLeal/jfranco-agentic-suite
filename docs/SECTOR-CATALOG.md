# Sector Catalog — where the suite fits across the whole economy

The J.Franco suite is sector-agnostic: the same listening, traffic-light,
reporting, and grading loop applies everywhere. This catalog maps the full
economic spectrum (based on the NAICS/ISIC classification used by census
bureaus) and the social layers, so no sector is forgotten when offering the
product.

## Primary sector

| Activity | Nearest template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Agriculture, farming, livestock | General (or Logistics) | Field reports, harvest logistics, supplier calls |
| Mining and extraction | General (or Construction) | Safety logs, equipment requests |
| Fishing and aquaculture | Logistics | Catch logging, delivery coordination |

## Secondary sector (industry)

| Activity | Nearest template | Example |

## Rule of thumb

When no template fits, use **General**: read-only work is GREEN, anything
with consequences for another person is YELLOW, and anything irreversible,
financial, or legal is RED. The human decides — always.
