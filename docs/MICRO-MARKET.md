## The zero-hardware truth (no Ollama needed)

The veil PII 2.1, the validators and the vault are DETERMINISTIC
plain Node.js — 85 MB RAM, 0.2 ms CPU, on any 8-year-old machine.
Without Ollama, the ladder skips the local rung in 0 ms (the veil still
runs 100% on the client's machine) and the cloud cost rises by only
+$0.21 CAD/month (600 tasks) to +$0.87 (2,500 tasks). The margin stays
99.6-99.9%. ONE executable, ONE supervisor: the difference between the
gym owner and the 15-employee restaurant is the uiProfile object
(staffCount === 1 hides the wall, the handoff and the mesh) — NEVER a
second app.
