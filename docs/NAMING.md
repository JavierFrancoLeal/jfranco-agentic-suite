# NAMING — the Sor Juana nomenclature of Trasunto

*The house standard for naming everything the company builds. Based on
the neologisms of Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz in Primero Sueño (1692): a
single intellectual narrative for the whole architecture — unlike
fragmented technical acronyms. Every new product, model or module MUST
take its name from this system.*

## The epistemology (why this system)

In Primero Sueño, the soul tries to grasp the whole universe at once
(pure intuition) and FAILS from data overload — exactly like a language
model that hallucinates when its context window saturates. The intellect
then activates specific mechanisms. Each mechanism is a product line:

| Mechanism in the poem | Product line |
|---|---|
| The step-by-step ascent | The Discurso reasoning series |
| The lantern that reveals ships in the fog | Fanal — vision/patterns |
| The chaining of ideas into one system | Engarze — orchestration |
| Compressing a universe into one sign | Cifra — code/embeddings |
| The needle that decides the exact balance | El Fiel — governance/HITL |

## The market advantage

Unlike pre-Hispanic terms or generic anglicisms, this family comes from
cultured Latin roots with direct equivalents in the three languages of
North America: Fanal (*fanal* / *beacon*), Cifra (*chiffre* / *cipher*),
Ápice (*apex*), Discurso (*discours* / *discourse*). A corporate client
in Quebec, Toronto or Mexico City perceives technical rigor and a deeply
thought architecture.
