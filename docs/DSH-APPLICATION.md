# DSH-APPLICATION — the angles and the draft answers (2026-11-08)

*The Feishu form is behind the accounts.feishu.cn login wall (the
fields cannot be read from here). The angles below are drafted to be
copied into the form once the director opens it. Thinking like
DeepSeek: what would make a DeepSeek reviewer stop and select this
project?*

## The five angles that make the project SELECTABLE

1. **DeepSeek as the WORKHORSE tier, not the toy** — the 4-brain ladder
   routes ~95% of the cloud load to DeepSeek V4 (the rescue tier is a
   competitor at <5%). The message DS wants to read: "DeepSeek = the
   reliable, affordable core; the expensive rival = the occasional
   rescue."
2. **Cost-efficient engineering as the showcase** — 75-80% of the
   tasks run on the client's local brain; the veil and the validators
   are deterministic (no token waste); the calibrated cost per task is
   ~$0.001-0.01. A credit grant here goes 100x further than in a
   typical wrapper — DS funds a demonstration of ITS OWN efficiency.
3. **The ecosystem contribution** — 14 plugins published on npm for
   the DSH store (the organs: workflow, decisions, accountability,
   audit, forensics, docfactory, constitution, branches, doctrine,
   apices...) + the monorepo with the compatibility contracts. The
   applicant BUILDS on DSH, not just consumes it.
4. **The responsible-AI angle DS promotes** — the human signs, the
   bilateral accountability, the friction honesty (the app refuses to
   sell itself to 30% of prospects), the Loi 25 sovereignty. This is
   the "trustworthy deployment" story the form wants.
5. **The real beta NOW** — the credits power the first 6 Quebec beta
   clients (a CPA, a lawyer, a gym, a plex, a clinician, a daycare) in
   the next 4 weeks: a measurable, dated, real-world outcome, not a
   roadmap.
