## Imbrication — does the conjunction make noise or work?

The conjunction WORKS when the layers read the same truth: the doctrine
feeds the brain, the brain consults the constitution and the memory,
the hands act under the semáforo, the organs grade the records, the
gates verify the packaging. The noise lives in ONE place: the vestigial
organs (8 plugins from the early anatomy that no skill uses) and the
duplicated legacy docs. The rule of nature applies: an organism that
carries dead weight tires faster than one that sheds it.

## Efficiency verdict

- The core (server + 3 lines + constitution + memory + gates) is LEAN:
  one file, one face, ten languages, zero dependencies in the bridge.
- The tooling (badge, screen-read, drive-step) is now disciplined by
  the driver protocol.
- The waste to shed: 8 vestigial organs + the docs not in the MAP.

## Antifragility check

Every failure this session became a lesson in the Ledger and a guard in
the gates (the heart travels, the watcher's echo, the badge's end, the
keyboard's layout). That is antifragility by design: the stressor makes
the organism stronger — like a muscle that grows from the load, and
like the body's redundancy: two brains, two plugs, two worlds.

## The verdict (create / keep / prune)

- CREATE: nothing new until demand asks — the constitution organ is the
  last institutional piece for now.
- KEEP: the core, the 3 lines, the 9 active organs + constitution, the
  gates, the MAP.
- PRUNE: retire the 8 vestigial organs from the catalog (archive, never
  delete); mark legacy docs as superseded in the MAP.
