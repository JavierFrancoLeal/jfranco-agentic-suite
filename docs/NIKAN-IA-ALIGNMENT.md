# Trasunto × Fanal — alignment analysis

*Analysis of the business plan "Plan_affaires_Nikan_IA.docx" (v5.2, July
2026, SADC Charlevoix) against everything built in this repository.
2026-11-03 · J.Franco.*

## What the plan is

A professional, complete consulting-services business plan in French for
the Charlevoix region: **Trasunto** — the human link between AI tools and
PMEs. Positioning: "L'humain au sommet · L'IA en soutien". Three revenue
sources (implantation · managed services · applied innovation), five
services, a 6-month antifragile pilot, viability at ≈ $86–96k CAD/year,
and a conditional ask to SADC Charlevoix ($25k loan + $20k personal).

## The one-line verdict

> The plan is the BUSINESS CASE for the product we built, and the product
> is the PROOF that makes the plan credible. They were written apart and
> belong together: **Trasunto = the company; Fanal = the product.**

## What the plan teaches the blueprint

1. **Viability numbers** — M2 of the blueprint gets real figures: charges
   ≈ $36.4k, salary $50–60k, threshold $86–96k, three client scenarios.
2. **The pilot is the engine** — the plan structures what the blueprint
   called M1: one business, 6 months, operational tariff, real data.
3. **The name architecture** — company Trasunto (inc.), product Le
   Veilleux, suite J.Franco. One organism, three names, each with a job:
   the company sells, the product delivers, the suite is the engine.

## Updates to make in the plan (5 edits, not a rewrite)

1. Add the product: a section "La démonstration : Fanal" — the
   supervisor, the register, the organs — the pilot no longer starts
   from zero.
2. Strengthen the antifragile section with lived proof: the five
   uncertainty families, the 23 lessons, the guard layers (Knowledge Ledger).
3. Align Service 2 pricing with the product: per-worker / per-site
   monthly (the register's Z-report justifies the invoice).
4. Add one persona per sector pack we already ship.
5. Keep the name Trasunto for the company and SADC — Fanal stays
   the product brand.

## Conclusion

Nothing in the plan contradicts the product; everything in the plan
lacks the product. The next artifact — the SADC one-pager — should be
written as the plan's annex 13.2, and the pilot agreement as its
operational annex. The 100 hours were not wasted: they built the
business; this repository built the proof.

## 2026-11-03 — Deep re-read: the nuggets, the traps, and the future

### ADOPT — the plan is BETTER than our dev here (5 gold nuggets)

1. **The subsidy arsenal (4.4)** — CRIC: refundable 30% tax credit on the
   first $1M of eligible spending; PARI-CNRC non-repayable funding for IA
   adoption; FRCN regional calls. This is a SALES argument: the client
   rarely pays everything from their pocket. Add to the grille/one-pager.
2. **The HITL decision map (6.3)** — the plan's "carte de décision HITL
   (où s'arrête l'IA, où commence l'humain)" is EXACTLY our
   decision-router tiers (GREEN/YELLOW/RED) in consulting language. Use
   the plan's vocabulary when talking to clients.
3. **The lead magnet (5.4)** — the free guide "10 façons simples
   d'utiliser l'IA dans votre PME, sans perdre le contrôle". We have all
   the content; produce it — it is the funnel's second step.
4. **Internal policies (6.4)** — Loi 25 (Québec) compliance, Canada-hosted
   data (Hydro-Québec sovereignty), fixed-price transparency, the HITL
   clause in every mandate. Adopt verbatim as our policy page.
5. **Pilot KPIs + public case study (9.2)** — heures économisées,
   productivité, satisfaction, and a case study VALIDATED by the pilot
   owner. Our register/Z-report gives us the evidence feed.

### AVOID — the plan is WORSE than our dev here

1. Consulting hours as the core business — the product (subscription) is
   the core; the five services become the onboarding ladder.
2. "En personne obligatoire" for discovery — keep in-person as a regional
   asset, not a rule (hybrid for non-Charlevoix).
3. All-out vagueness (no durations/percentages anywhere) — right for the
   SADC document, wrong for internal execution: the blueprint's exit
   conditions are the measurable version.
4. Serving zero-tech clients with the product directly — they need the
   implantation service first; do not promise self-serve to them.
5. The name Nikan — already resolved with Trasunto (also removes the
   Indigenous-name validation step).
