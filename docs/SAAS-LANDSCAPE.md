# SAAS-LANDSCAPE — the incumbents Fanal meets in every sector (2026-11-07)

*The competitive map: which SaaS dominate each sector, WHY companies
use them, and where Fanal substitutes them vs. where it sits in front
of them as the douane with the semáforo. Sources: the Québec accounting
comparative (QuickBooks 62% of Canada — Boulet Stratégies TI, Dec 2025)
and the Canadian POS rankings (CleverPays 2026).*

## Why companies use SaaS (the four universal reasons)

1. **Compliance** — the TPS/TVQ is native (QuickBooks, Acomba): the
   penalty of a tax error is the real price.
2. **The accountant's ecosystem** — "your accountant uses QuickBooks,
   so you use QuickBooks": the compatibility with the professional is
   worth more than any feature.
3. **The transaction core** — the POS, the card payments, the
   inventory: systems of record.
4. **The switching cost** — migration costs 10-20 hours minimum; once
   installed, the SaaS stays.

## Where Fanal substitutes, and where it sits in front

- Fanal does NOT substitute the TRANSACTION CORES (the POS, the books
  of record, the payments): those systems hold the money trail and the
  compliance the client cannot risk.
- Fanal SUBSTITUTES the LABOR BETWEEN the systems: the double entry,
  the unanswered messages, the form-filling, the drafting for the
  professional, the handoff of the shift — the 15 branch agents + the
  three lines + the sessions. That is the "middleware of the 4th
  industrial revolution" — the glue no SaaS sells.
- Fanal ABSORBS the light tools (the appointment links, the feedback
  forms, the shared notes) when the client's pack makes them redundant.

## The species verdict (2026-11-07): substitute, complement, or sit in front

The direct question: does Fanal substitute and complement ALL the SaaS
services? The honest answer: Fanal is a DIFFERENT SPECIES — it is not a
system of record, it is the LABOR LAYER. A system of record holds the
canonical data, the money trail and the compliance (TPS/TVQ, the
Barreau, the payment rails); Fanal holds NONE of that — it prepares,
guards and proposes AROUND the records.

| The SaaS | Fanal's verdict |
|---|---|
| QuickBooks/Acomba (the books, the tax filing) | COMPLEMENT — Fanal prepares the entries; the books stay theirs |
| Lightspeed/Maitre D (the POS, the payments) | SITS IN FRONT — the semáforo guards what enters the POS |
| Clio (the Barreau case system) | COMPLEMENT — Fanal drafts the dossiers; Clio stays the record |
| Cloudbeds/Booking (the channels) | SITS IN FRONT — Fanal answers the guests; the PMS stays |
| Calendly/Notion-lite (the light tools) | SUBSTITUTE — the pack absorbs them when it makes sense |

Could a client accustomed to SaaS migrate easily? NO — and that is the
MOAT: Fanal requires ZERO migration because it does not replace the
system of record. The client keeps QuickBooks and the POS (their 10-20
hours of migration stays untouched) and Fanal becomes the workforce
that feeds them. "Fanal does more" is true in ONE dimension: the LABOR
(the 15 agents, the bilateral accountability, the semáforo, the
multi-brain ladder, the sovereignty) — no SaaS sells that. In the
RECORD dimension, the incumbents stay — by design.
