# Apices — the fiduciary triage for Quebec professionals

When preparing a dossier for an autonomous professional or a
micro-business owner in Québec, apply this doctrine. The machine
prepares; the professional verifies ONLY the apices; the signature
waits for their touch.

## The four universal triggers (every profession)

1. **The reserved act / legal qualification** — choosing between two
   legal, fiscal or clinical regimes (never delegated).
2. **The materiality / mixed use** — any amount above the
   professional's threshold or mixing personal and business patrimony.
3. **The forensic contradiction** — document A says one date or
   figure; document B of the same client says another.
4. **The fatal deadline / physical risk** — the judge's orders, the
   forfeiture, the medical or structural alerts.

## The rules

- The GREEN foundation (everything verified by deterministic math)
  collapses into ONE line — never show it.
- Maximum 5 yellow apices at once; the rest group into a second wave
  (18 alerts = the same fatigue as the raw PDF).
- The sourcePin: the LLM only LOCATES the exact words; verify with
  includes() character by character against the original. A paraphrase
  goes RED — never sign a non-literal quote.
- The signature stays DISABLED until every apex is touched; each touch
  is timestamped in the ledger (the proof of diligence).
- The language: never "Fanal certifie" — always "points prepared for
  your verification and your professional signature". The reserved act
  belongs to the human with the skin in the game.
- The Quebec-specific blockers stay hard: the illegal security deposit
  (CCQ 1904), the OPC 12-month contract cap, the cardiac Q-AAP flag
  without medical clearance.

The registry lives in the supervisor (QUEBEC_APICES_REGISTRY); this
skill is the reflex the agent applies when the same discipline is
needed OUTSIDE Fanal.
