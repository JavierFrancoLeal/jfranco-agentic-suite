# Doctrine — the active constitution of the house

Every Fanal/Trasunto task obeys these laws. The docs are the full
constitution; this skill is the ACTIVE subset the agent applies.

## The laws

1. **The one-sentence test** — every screen must pass: "could the owner
   explain it to their employee in one sentence?" A screen that fails
   is not finished.
2. **The zero-CPU rule** — the interface idles at ~0% CPU/GPU: no
   continuous animations, no heavy gradients, the 380px side-strip,
   the GREEN silence. Fanal lives on a BUSY machine.
3. **The friction honesty** — Fanal is the exoskeleton for the heavy
   day, not the uniform for every day. Sell where the numbers say yes
   (volume, recurring flows, compliance, professional prep); SAY IT
   when they say no (the semi-empty restaurant is CALM_STANDBY). The
   measurable verdict lives in /api/pilot/health and /api/friction/state.
4. **The wash order** — doctrine → data model → brain + gates → the
   face + the one-sentence test → packaging + the heart-travels check →
   the business layer. Never inverted.
5. **The species verdict** — Fanal is the LABOR layer, not the system
   of record: it substitutes the operational layer (80%) and
   complements the closed rails (MEV-WEB, RAMQ, EDI). Never certify a
   state rail; never say "throw away your Acomba" — say "we import your
   catalog and your accountant gets the CSV squared to the cent".
6. **The diminishing-returns rule** — the tooling serves the product;
   the meta-tooling is time-boxed; two failures switch the channel; one
   heavy operation per turn; never poll the same state twice.
7. **The human signs** — the machine executes what carries no
   consequences; the human signs what does. The skin follows the
   signature; the bilateral accountability extends to the certified
   professional.
8. **The one house language** — every plugin, doc, commit message, and
   shared artifact is written in English. Runtime strings facing the
   human may be localized through `locale/*.json`, but the canonical
   source is English. Never ship Spanish (or French) in a shared
   package. Regexes that match real-world button labels (the cockpit's
   `FORBIDDEN` set) are the one exception — they stay multilingual.
